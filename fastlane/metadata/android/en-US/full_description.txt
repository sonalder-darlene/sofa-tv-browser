Sofa is a lightweight web browser made for the TV remote, with a mouse pointer, tabs and a start page of your own. It has no ads, analytics, accounts or servers of its own.

Remote-control browsing
• The D-pad moves a mouse pointer that speeds up while held and stops dead on release.
• OK clicks, holding OK while moving drags, and hover works, so hover menus open.
• Hold OK on a link or image to open it in a new tab or another app, copy, share or download it, or set a picture as the start page background.
• Pushing the pointer against a screen edge scrolls, and there is a scroll mode and a D-pad mode.
• The Back key opens a menu: address, tabs, Watch, navigation, find in page, bookmarks, history, ad blocking per site, "Browse as" (desktop, mobile, iPad, TV or your own), zoom and settings. Its buttons can be reordered and hidden.
• Game pads (Xbox, PlayStation, 8BitDo and others) work like in a console browser.
• TalkBack, the TV's screen reader, reads Sofa's menus, settings and dialogs.

Tabs and your start page
• Up to 10 tabs at the top of the menu. Only the tab you look at runs, and one left alone for 10 minutes is unloaded until you pick it. Private browsing has its own tabs.
• The start page has your quick-access tiles, your most visited sites (worked out on the TV) and up to 48 bookmarks. Reorder and hide its rows and buttons, and give it a colour, a gradient or your own picture.
• Sofa can open on the start page, on a web page you choose, or where you left off.
• Import and export bookmarks as the file every desktop browser uses, and back up your settings, lists and bookmarks to one password-protected file.

Video
• Watch: one press of Play finds the page's main video, even in an embedded player, and plays it fullscreen.
• Sofa Player plays the site's stream in its own player when it can: 4K at full resolution, HDR, and a choice of quality, sound and subtitles that it remembers.
• Fullscreen controls for every video: 10-second seek, pause, speed, subtitles, Video info and picture-in-picture.
• Play in another app sends the stream, subtitles and position to VLC, Just Player or others.
• Match frame rate (experimental) switches the TV to the video's rate, such as 24 for films. The screen stays on during video, and long films continue where you stopped.

Search and downloads
• Pick your engine at first start: Brave Search, DuckDuckGo, Ecosia, Google, Kagi, MetaGer, Mojeek, Murena Find, Qwant, Startpage, Swisscows, Uruky, or any address. Voice search, and suggestions from your bookmarks and history.
• Downloads pause, resume and carry on in the background.

Blocking and safety
• Ads and trackers are blocked on the TV with filter lists in uBlock Origin, Adblock Plus or hosts format, which also hide the empty boxes ads leave. One switch adds EasyList and EasyPrivacy.
• Pop-ups are blocked even from ad networks no list knows yet. "Allow once" opens one you wanted.
• Site filters (off until you choose) block whole sites by category, such as adult, gambling, unsafe sites or drugs, or with a list of your own.
• A parental PIN (off until you set one) guards Settings and the blockers.
• Custom DNS (off until you choose) looks sites up over DNS over HTTPS, with services ranked from most to least private.

Privacy
• Third-party cookies are blocked, and Sofa blends in as desktop Chrome, without Do Not Track.
• Private browsing has its own tabs, and its cookies and site data are erased when it ends.
• A page you leave stays quiet: no sound, videos paused, nothing opened behind your back.
• Pages never get the camera, microphone, location or your files.
• You choose how long history is kept and whether protected (DRM) video plays.
• No crash reporting, remote configuration or cloud backup.

Updates
Copies from GitHub can check for updates if you say yes at first start; app store copies never do.

Sofa uses the TV's system WebView. It needs Android 7.0 or newer and is made for Android TV and Google TV.
