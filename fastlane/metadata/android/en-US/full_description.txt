Sofa is a lightweight web browser made for the TV remote, with a mouse pointer, a menu on the Back key and a home screen of your own sites. It has no ads, analytics, accounts or servers of its own.

Remote-control browsing
• The D-pad moves a mouse pointer that speeds up while held and stops dead on release. A quick tap nudges it a few pixels.
• OK clicks, holding OK while moving drags, and hover works, so hover menus open.
• Pushing the pointer against a screen edge scrolls the page, or whatever is under the pointer.
• Scroll mode, and a D-pad mode for sites built for keyboard navigation.
• The Back key opens a menu: address, Watch, navigation, find in page, bookmarks, history, ad blocking per site, "Browse as" (desktop, mobile, iPad, Smart TV or your own user agent), zoom and settings.

Home screen
• Your own quick-access tiles, which you can add, edit and reorder.
• Most visited sites, worked out on the TV from your history, and your recent bookmarks.

Video
• Watch: one press of Play (or Watch in the menu) finds the page's main video, even inside an embedded player, and plays it fullscreen.
• Fullscreen video gets a control bar, even for videos embedded from other sites: back and forward 10 seconds, play/pause, a scrub bar, playback speed, subtitles where the video has them, Video info and picture-in-picture on TVs that offer it.
• Play in another app: send the video to VLC, mpv, MX Player or another player. Android asks once and Sofa remembers; hold OK on the button to choose again.
• In fullscreen, sites see your TV's real resolution, so on a 4K TV they can send their 4K stream.
• Match frame rate (experimental, off by default): on TVs that offer several refresh rates, the TV switches to the video's frame rate in fullscreen, such as 24 per second for films, for smoother motion.
• The screen stays on while a video plays, and long films offer to continue where you stopped.

Search
• At first start you choose the search engine yourself: Brave Search, DuckDuckGo, Ecosia, Google, Kagi, MetaGer, Mojeek, Murena Find, Qwant, Startpage, Swisscows or Uruky, or later any address you like.
• Suggestions come from your bookmarks and history. Your engine's own suggestions are only used if you turn them on.
• Voice search runs in Sofa's own search screen, and can be turned off.

Downloads
• Up to three at once, with pause and resume, automatic retries, and downloads that carry on in the background.

Privacy
• Ads, trackers and pop-ups are blocked on the TV itself, using the StevenBlack hosts list and HaGeZi's Pop-Up Ads list. Third-party cookies are blocked.
• Few browsers send Do Not Track, and desktop Chrome is the most common browser, so Sofa never sends Do Not Track and shows a desktop Chrome user agent by default. Both help it blend in.
• Private browsing runs in a separate WebView profile where the TV's WebView supports it.
• Pages never get the camera, microphone, location or your files.
• You choose how long history is kept (7, 30 or 90 days, always or never) and whether protected (DRM) video asks first, always plays or never plays.
• No crash reporting, remote configuration or cloud backup.

Updates
Copies installed from GitHub can check GitHub for new versions, but only if you say yes at first start. Copies installed from an app store never see this option.

Sofa uses the TV's system WebView. It needs Android 7.0 or newer and is made for Android TV and Google TV.
